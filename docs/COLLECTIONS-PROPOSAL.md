# Proposed collections for Wesee / Zeelool frames

Written for: the client, who will approve or change this list before collections are created in Shopify.

**Status: proposal, not yet approved.** No collections have been created. Once approved, the list can be used to build the collections and their automatic rules.

## Proposed 14 collections

| # | Collection | Covers | Suggested rule (once the feed has the field) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Round Frames | Round and circular shapes | Shape = Round |
| 2 | Square Frames | Square and boxy shapes | Shape = Square |
| 3 | Rectangle Frames | Rectangular shapes | Shape = Rectangle |
| 4 | Aviator Frames | Teardrop, aviator-style | Shape = Aviator |
| 5 | Cat-Eye Frames | Upswept cat-eye | Shape = Cat-Eye |
| 6 | Oversized Frames | Large-lens styles | Shape = Oversized |
| 7 | Browline Frames | Browline and half-rim | Shape = Browline |
| 8 | Titanium Frames | Titanium construction | Material = Titanium |
| 9 | Acetate Frames | Acetate / plastic | Material = Acetate |
| 10 | Lightweight Frames | Lightweight and flexible | Tag = Lightweight |
| 11 | Blue Light Glasses | Blue-light filtering lenses | Tag = Blue Light |
| 12 | Men's Frames | Men's styles | Gender = Men |
| 13 | Women's Frames | Women's styles | Gender = Women |
| 14 | Kids Frames | Children's sizes | Gender = Kids |

A frame can belong to more than one collection (for example, Round and Titanium).

## Open questions for the client

1. Does the supplier feed include shape, material, gender and lens-type fields? If yes, the rules above can be automatic. If not, the sync must set them manually or from a mapping.
2. Are these 14 the right categories? Any that should be merged, renamed or added?
3. Should collections show only ACTIVE (published) products? Recommended: yes. New products stay hidden until the client publishes them.

## What happens after approval

- Create the 14 collections in Shopify with the rules above.
- Add a shape, material and gender field to the feed mapping, or add a tag at sync time, so the rules have data to match.
